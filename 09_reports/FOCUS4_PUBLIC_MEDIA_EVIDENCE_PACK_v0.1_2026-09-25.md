# Focus-4 Public Media Evidence Pack v0.1

**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Scope:** Office Cat / My Perfect Hotel / Big Farm Homestead / The Cozy Florist

## What this pack does

This pack adds a reproducible public-media evidence layer to the SIMS benchmark.

It does **not** copy or redistribute full third-party gameplay videos. Instead it stores:
- source URL;
- product;
- market/platform;
- title;
- capture/use purpose;
- evidence strength;
- copyright handling rule;
- status/notes.

For analysis, the preferred workflow is:
1. use public videos for historical / D7+ / hard-to-reach states;
2. cite timestamp and source URL;
3. use current official Store screenshots and event/version pages for current-state validation;
4. use our own device/emulator recording for current FTUE whenever possible;
5. save only permitted screenshots/annotated excerpts in the public repo.

## Current public gameplay coverage

- Office Cat: public no-commentary Android gameplay found; separate Android/iOS walkthrough also available.
- My Perfect Hotel: no-commentary YouTube Playables gameplay found; mobile Store evidence remains the source of truth for mobile product/monetization.
- Big Farm Homestead: long-form mobile walkthrough found, plus current US App Store / Google Play evidence.
- The Cozy Florist: public gameplay walkthrough found, plus current US App Store / Google Play evidence.

## Analysis rule

A video proves only what is visibly present in the recorded version.
It does not prove:
- current version parity;
- US-specific monetization;
- D1/D7 unlock timing unless the recording documents it;
- paid/organic acquisition;
- actual campaign performance.

All derived FTUE/feature observations must keep the source URL + timestamp.
