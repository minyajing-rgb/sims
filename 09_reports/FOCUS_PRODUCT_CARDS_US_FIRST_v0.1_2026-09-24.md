## 1. Gossip Harbor

### 1. Identity / market
- **OBSERVED:** US App Store title `Gossip Harbor®: Merge & Story`; publisher Microfun; first released 2022-07.
- **OBSERVED:** Product promise combines merge food orders, restaurant restoration/decor, relationship drama and mystery.
- **US source:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/gossip-harbor-merge-story/id1623318294

### 2. Business trend
- **CALCULATED / Global collection scope:** visible 2022-07→2026-08 downloads ≈168.2M; IAP ≈$1.376B.
- **CALCULATED:** latest downloads ≈83.9% of visible peak; 2026-08 revenue is the visible peak.
- **INFERRED:** mature product is still capable of reacquisition and monetization expansion; this is not proof of one event causing growth.

### 3. Art / visual identity
- **OBSERVED:** illustrated 2D story scenes + highly readable merge board + coastal restaurant ownership layer.
- **OBSERVED:** character cast, food objects and restoration spaces all create ad/screenshot surfaces.
- **Production read:** Heavy narrative/event illustration burden, partially offset by reusable merge board and themed-event wrapper.

### 4. Core gameplay
`Merge items → fulfill orders → earn progression → restore/decorate restaurant → unlock story → return`
- **Core:** merge/order fulfillment.
- **Meta:** restaurant restoration + cast/story.
- **LiveOps wrapper:** themed point/exploration events, collections and exclusive decoration rewards.

### 5. FTUE
- **TBD:** exact first 30-second / T+5m / T+10m timings require recorded gameplay capture.
- Store description must not be treated as timed FTUE evidence.

### 6. Progression / Meta / Side
- **OBSERVED:** restaurant restoration and decoration.
- **OBSERVED:** character relationships / mystery story progression.
- **OBSERVED:** card/collection and rotating event layers are visible in store/review evidence.
- **TBD:** exact unlock timeline and D1/D3/D7/D14 system exposure.

### 7. Economy / monetization
- **OBSERVED / review signal:** energy is a major session limiter; higher-level orders are repeatedly described as expensive in energy.
- **INFERRED:** energy, order complexity and collection/event completion create monetization pressure surfaces.
- **TBD:** US offer prices, payer segmentation, purchase frequency and cohort LTV.

### 8. LiveOps
- **OBSERVED:** US App Store states a **new episode every Friday**.
- **OBSERVED:** visible versions rotate themed events such as Swan Lake, Palace of Prosperity, Ancient Egypt, Dollhouse of Dreams, raccoon wasteland and lunar-fantasy themes.
- **OBSERVED:** recurring event pattern = collect points → unlock exclusive decorations / event completion.
- **Operating model:** weekly narrative + roughly biweekly themed event reskin.

### 9. Versions / reviews / quality
- **OBSERVED:** high client cadence around narrative/event refresh.
- **OBSERVED review themes:** energy scarcity, hard-to-complete collections/events, expensive progression; some US reviews also flag misleading-ad expectation gaps and quality/QA frustrations.
- **Rule:** complaints are early-warning signals, not confirmed bugs or causal proof.

### 10. UA / promotion / creative
- **INFERRED from product surfaces:** strongest credible hooks are relationship drama, mystery, merge satisfaction, restaurant before/after and event fantasy worlds.
- **Risk:** unrelated rescue/survival creatives can produce product-promise mismatch if the install experience does not support them.

### 11. Production burden
- Character/story: Heavy.
- Environment/restaurant restoration: Medium-Heavy.
- Weekly narrative operations: Heavy.
- Event engine reuse: Medium-High.
- Small-team feasibility: Medium only with strict templates and reuse.

### 12. Trend × Cause
- **E1:** 2026-07 Global downloads +61.3% MoM and IAP +8.1% overlaps visible event/version activity.
- **Counter evidence:** the same high cadence continues when August downloads normalize while revenue grows; no paid/organic or spend evidence.
- **Interpretation:** event cadence is part of the operating system, not sufficient causal attribution.

### 13. Learn
- Weekly story can be an operating cadence, not just a content drop.
- One reusable event shell can carry many narrative skins.
- Ownership/decor gives merge output a visible persistent meaning.

### 14. Do not copy
- Bespoke weekly story/event art without a templated content pipeline.
- Monetization pressure so high that session energy becomes the dominant review complaint.
- Creative promises that differ from the actual installed core.

### 15. Ops Cat implication
- Test `Mow/Clean → progress points → themed decor reward → short NPC beat` on one reusable event engine.
- Keep creative hook and installed core identical.

### 16. Evidence gaps
- US-only monthly KPI; paid/organic/source mix; historical creative/spend; timed FTUE; US offer screenshots/prices; full review time series.

## 2. Hay Day

### 1. Identity / market
- **OBSERVED:** long-running Supercell farming game; US App Store available.
- **US source:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hay-day/id506627515

### 2. Business trend
- **CALCULATED / Global collection scope:** 2021-09→2026-08 visible downloads ≈166.4M; IAP ≈$634.5M.
- **CALCULATED:** latest downloads ≈48.6% of visible 5Y peak while latest revenue ≈96.5% of visible revenue peak.
- **INFERRED:** one of the cleanest examples of acquisition/revenue decoupling in a mature farming game.

### 3. Art / visual identity
- **OBSERVED:** isometric stylized 3D farm, iconic animals, production machines and highly legible object silhouettes.
- **Brand read:** strong one-second category recognition without relying on realistic detail.
- **Production read:** heavy overall breadth, but highly modular and reusable.

### 4. Core gameplay
`Plant → harvest → process in machines → fulfill truck/boat/town orders or trade → earn → expand → return`
- **Core remains central** even after years of Meta expansion.
- Ownership is the farm itself; new machines/crops/animals deepen rather than replace the production core.

### 5. FTUE
- **TBD:** timed FTUE capture not yet recorded.
- **OBSERVED from release notes:** Supercell explicitly tests unlock-flow improvements (e.g. Boat unlock flow), showing onboarding/progression is actively optimized even in a mature product.

### 6. Progression / Meta / Side
- Farm expansion, machines, town, fishing, Valley, Neighborhood, Derby, design/collection features.
- Seasonal and community systems wrap the evergreen production loop rather than replace it.

### 7. Economy / monetization
- **OBSERVED official D2C:** Supercell Store sells Farm Pass and store-exclusive offers with loyalty/bonus rewards.
- **OBSERVED official subscription:** Farm Pass Bundle Subscription renews each season in selected regions and includes bonus pass points, capacity perks and a choice/fortune crate.
- **OBSERVED:** subscription eligibility requires Level 11+; availability is region-limited.
- **Interpretation:** D2C + recurring pass is a durable monetization/loyalty architecture, but availability must not be generalized to every market.
- Official sources:
  - https://store.supercell.com/hayday/farm-pass-subscription
  - https://support.supercell.com/supercell-store/en/articles/farm-pass-bundle-sub.html

### 8. LiveOps / external promotion
- **OBSERVED official campaign:** Jan 12–Feb 13, 2026 Neighborhood Nurture Event with Lance Bass; players grow a neighborhood tree by normal farm tasks, tied to a real-world tree-growing milestone with Rainforest Alliance.
- **OBSERVED:** community target of 1M players reaching Level 5 was connected to a real-world tree-growing commitment.
- Source: https://supercell.com/en/news/hayday-lancebass/

### 9. Versions / reviews / quality
- **OBSERVED:** release notes show QoL work, limited tests, new crops/animals and Design Festival improvements.
- **OBSERVED:** product experiments are often rolled out to subsets rather than treated as all-player permanent features immediately.

### 10. UA / creative / promotion
- Credible hooks: satisfying harvest/production, farm ownership, iconic animals, neighborhood cooperation, seasonal/brand/community campaigns.
- External celebrity/community campaigns sit on top of an intact evergreen core.

### 11. Production burden
- Character: Medium-Heavy.
- Environment: Heavy but modular.
- LiveOps: Heavy, with high reuse.
- Backend/social: Heavy.
- Small-team feasibility: Medium as a system reference, low if copying mature breadth.

### 12. Trend × Cause
- **E1 counterexample:** large anniversary/content packages do not automatically create large acquisition spikes.
- **Interpretation:** mature retention/monetization and brand/community loops can matter more than a single update’s install lift.

### 13. Learn
- Keep a durable production/order core alive for years.
- Build D2C and pass/loyalty around established value, not as a substitute for a weak core.
- Use normal gameplay tasks inside community events to reduce content-production burden.

### 14. Do not copy
- Mature feature breadth in an MVP.
- Assume celebrity/brand activation equals paid-UA ROI.
- Treat region-limited subscription availability as universal.

### 15. Ops Cat implication
- Build one evergreen home/garden production loop first.
- Later wrap community milestones/pass around actions the player already does.
- Consider D2C only after repeatable value and payer behavior are proven.

### 16. Evidence gaps
- US-only monthly KPI/source mix, actual US subscription availability, payer mix, paid media/spend, timed FTUE.

- Operational reliability around paid event surfaces must be tracked as part of monetization design.

- Multiple farms/biomes before one core farm loop is proven.
- Infer payer retention from calendar revenue hold.
- Run a monetized event cadence without robust event-recovery/support playbooks.

### 15. Ops Cat implication

- Include event recovery / compensation / persistence rules in the system PRD from day one.

# Cross-product synthesis

## A. What changed
- Product-card level evidence now distinguishes **core**, **meta**, **LiveOps**, **economy**, **quality risk**, **production burden** and **Trend×Cause** for four benchmark roles.

## B. What may explain it
- Gossip Harbor: reusable narrative/event machine supports continuous refresh (**E1, not causal**).
- Big Farm Homestead: dense Festival/Pass/collection stack is consistent with monetization resilience (**E1, not retention proof**).
- Hay Day: mature evergreen production + D2C/pass/loyalty architecture supports long-term value (**observed architecture; KPI causality not established**).

## C. What does NOT explain it
- Event cadence alone does not explain acquisition direction.
- Major content releases do not guarantee install growth.
- New city/event content does not necessarily reverse a decaying acquisition curve.

## D. Repeating patterns
1. Persistent ownership anchors long-term value.
2. Reusable event mother engines outperform one-off mini-game proliferation for production efficiency.
3. Quality/recovery around monetized events is a business-system requirement.
4. Mature games separate acquisition, monetization and community cycles.

## E. What Ops Cat should test
1. **Core A:** pure Mow/Clean satisfying action.
2. **Core B:** A + persistent garden/home ownership.
3. **Core C:** B + collection/NPC/story + one reusable event mother engine.
4. First event engine: `Task progress → event currency → milestone track → collection/decor reward → optional pass`.
5. Add event progress persistence/recovery/compensation specification before scaling paid event content.

## F. Still missing
- US-only monthly KPI; paid/organic/source mix; timed FTUE; historical creative/spend; offer/pricing screenshots; full historical reviews; event participation; annotated art screenshots.
