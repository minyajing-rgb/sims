Compensation taxonomy

Map incident type to recovery: entitlement restore, progression restore, event extension, claim replay, generic goodwill, or no compensation for display-only defects.

Ops Cat visual / first-30s rule · v2.5

0–30s

Show the real tactile verb and visible state change before introducing breadth. Hay Day's timed route is the cleanest current reference.

Store / ad bridge

Emotion can lead, but by second 5–10 the creative/store sequence should expose either the actual interaction or the persistent owned asset it changes.

Visual differentiation

Keep 70–90% mobile readability; spend the 10–30% authored layer on material, lighting, flora, story props and character/pet identity—not on UI density.

Ops Cat production-envelope decision · v2.6

MVP envelope

Default to Lean modular: one satisfying action, one owned space, modular areas, server-configurable LiveOps where practical.

Reuse rule

Build shared state/event/UI primitives early enough to reuse, but do not pre-build a platform for hypothetical future worlds.

Backend guardrail

Define save/rollback/compensation from day one; delay MMO-like social persistence until D7/D30 retention earns the heavier envelope.
