## A. What changed

This round improves current onboarding / progression evidence.

- **Heartopia:** first-hour order is character → controls → starter home/two plots → fishing → D.G.; current guides place Builder Tablet after about 10–15 minutes, Gardening around DG3 / Days 3–4, and broader hobby choice around DG5 / Days 5–7.
- **Hay Day:** Supercell states that the team rebuilt onboarding after identifying it as slow/clunky. 2026 release notes also changed early rewards, truck-order availability and field availability.
- **Big Farm Homestead:** Levels 1–3/4 are a mandatory linear tutorial; Level 2 introduces Cannery/processing; Level 3 introduces Market + Feed Mill + first contract, then releases broader autonomy.
- **Gossip Harbor:** current Sep-2026 guides expose the early friction layer: 100 energy cap, +1/2 minutes, 6–8 empty-tile safety buffer, pressure around Level 12, and a more complex generator-space game by ~Level 18.
- **The Cozy Florist:** current-ish Part-1 walkthrough route located; Boutique at Lv10; Flower Advancement at Florist Lv31 + flower Lv10.

## C. What does NOT explain it

Guide timing ≠ device timestamp. Day gates ≠ cohort retention. Release notes show product changes, not their measured business lift.

## D. Repeating patterns

1. Teach one productive verb before breadth.
2. Give ownership early, but delay identity/collection depth.
3. Use a controlled tutorial, then release autonomy.
4. The real early-game cliff often appears after D0.
5. Mature live games still rework onboarding.
