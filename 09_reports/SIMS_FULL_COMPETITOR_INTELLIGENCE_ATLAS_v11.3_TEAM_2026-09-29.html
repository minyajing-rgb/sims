Art

Fixed portrait rooms; flat hand-drawn/doodle 2D; simple anthropomorphic silhouettes; modular facility slots; themed full-set reskins/wallpapers. Rendering burden is Lean/Medium, while catalog/config burden is Heavy. 70–90% readability comes from mascot silhouette + owned room + swappable furniture, with more negative space recommended for Ops Cat.

Core

Promote → customers → staff/service → Cod/Tips/Stars → upgrade recipes/facilities/staff/promo → unlock scenes/customers → collect/decorate/story → return. Unlike tactile cooking games, the active repeated verb is Promote tapping; rewarded ads can substitute with Promo x15.

Focus Game

Animal Restaurant turns low-cost 2D illustration into a huge permanent collection graph: one soft mascot world → many semi-autonomous scenes → one Star/collection identity → persistent customers/letters/facilities/pets/performers. Event value persists after the event, but promotion taps, ads and scene currencies can convert a cozy game into a checklist.
