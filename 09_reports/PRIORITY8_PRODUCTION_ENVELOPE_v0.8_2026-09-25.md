- Six-month post-global milestone: >8M downloads, >KRW20B cumulative revenue, peak DAU >300K, average DAU around 250K during Jul-Nov 2024.
- US revenue share: 35% at the six-month milestone; 41% by Jul 2025.
- Guardrail: 8 people is the initial development team, not confirmed current live-team size.

- HISTORICAL: roughly half-dozen developers in early development.
- Publisher layer supplied analytics, LiveOps, A/B testing, creative testing and playable-performance tooling.
- Guardrail: publisher services are not developer headcount.

## Reuse-led 3D — Big Farm Homestead
- FIRST-PARTY CURRENT PROCESS: delivered in under one year.
- Shares code and many systems with Sunshine Island; title-specific assets/settings stay separate.
- Guardrail: reuse lowers engineering lead time but does not erase 3D art/QA burden.

## Persistent-world heavy — Heartopia
- HISTORICAL team signal: project once exceeded 150 people and later reduced to 80+ during long pre-launch development.
- CURRENT infrastructure: high-frequency player saves, sharded database, point-in-time restore, 30x faster recovery, 80+ monitoring metrics and minute-level horizontal scaling.
- Guardrail: historical team size is not relabeled as current 2026 staffing.

## Mature live-service — Township / Hay Day
- Township: roughly 20 at launch, around 130 by 2023.
- Hay Day: current 2026 team exceeds 60.
- These are mature live-service staffing references, not MVP staffing targets.
