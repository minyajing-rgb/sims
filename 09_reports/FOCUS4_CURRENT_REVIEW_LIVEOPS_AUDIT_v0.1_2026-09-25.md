## My Perfect Hotel
- **OBSERVED:** US App Store ~4.7 / 700K ratings.
- **OBSERVED:** visible version 1.37.1 dated 2026-09-11.
- **OBSERVED:** Barbie Dream Hotel continues to package characters/stories/skins + event pass + leaderboard over the stable hotel-service core.
- **OBSERVED:** visible IAP includes No Ads $9.99, gem packs from $1.99 upward and a permanent-pet offer.
- **REVIEW SIGNAL:** ad interruption, expensive upgrades and lag recur in public US reviews.
- **Product read:** strongest D0 readability benchmark; use theme/IP as a wrapper, not a replacement core.

## Office Cat
- **OBSERVED:** US App Store ~4.8 / 73K ratings.
- **OBSERVED:** visible version 1.0.80 dated 2026-08-31.
- **OBSERVED:** event surfaces include Halloween City 7 and Cat Mining Madness; recent versions also show Cat Day, Farm/Aquafarm rankings, anniversary exchange-shop content and Payment Challenge.
- **OBSERVED:** release notes explicitly fixed data-saving issues and seasonal/Mine-event ranking resets.
- **REVIEW SIGNAL:** gem scarcity and ad burden are repeated value/friction complaints.
- **Product read:** best lean modular-3D reference; persistence/recovery is a concrete QA requirement.

## Big Farm Homestead
- **OBSERVED:** US App Store ~4.7 / 3K ratings.
- **OBSERVED:** visible version 1.0.6384 dated 2026-09-09.
- **OBSERVED:** Deepwoods Festival = 4-week season + Festival Pass; Dino Drama is promoted for return; App Store event surfaces include Friends & Family collection content.
- **REVIEW SIGNAL:** a Sep 2 review flags unclear Chapter 10→11 progression; a Mar 10 review flags order-resource pressure and league discoverability.
- **Product read:** strongest current Focus-4 reference for ownership → region/production → social/guild → season/pass.
- **Ops implication:** communicate story gates explicitly and make social eligibility/discoverability obvious.

## The Cozy Florist
- **OBSERVED:** US App Store ~4.8 / 29K ratings.
- **OBSERVED:** version 1.0.66 was visible on 2026-09-24; Sep 2 and Sep 11 versions explicitly added new content/events.
- **OBSERVED:** current App Store event card promotes a Game Story Series.
- **POSITIVE REVIEW SIGNAL:** calm art, flower-shop fantasy, customer personalities and steady unlock/decor are praised.
- **NEGATIVE REVIEW SIGNAL:** public reviews flag monetization/event pressure and perceived mismatch between some promotional visuals and actual in-game models.
- **Product read:** narrow emotional fantasy can carry acquisition, but event/fashion/home layers must not crowd out the garden core.
