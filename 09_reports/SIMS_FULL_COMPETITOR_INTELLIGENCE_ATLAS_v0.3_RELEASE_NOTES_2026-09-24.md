## Current strongest new reads

1. **Event cadence is an operating architecture, not a causal explanation by itself.** Gossip Harbor and Township show high-density reusable content/event systems, but the same cadence can coexist with very different acquisition directions.
2. **Mature products separate acquisition and monetization cycles.** Hay Day, Township, Gossip Harbor and Office Cat all show download and revenue directions decoupling in visible windows.
3. **Big Farm Homestead remains the strongest new farming / cozy monetization teardown candidate.** Revenue holds much better than downloads after the May peak, while the visible client history shows repeatable Festival/Pass, limited event and collection engines. This remains a calendar-level business signal, not cohort retention/LTV.
4. **The Cozy Florist August rebound remains unexplained.** Its very high client-update cadence overlaps the rebound, but explicit content/event notes visible in the US App Store appear mainly in September; paid/organic/source-mix evidence is required before attribution.
5. **Ops Cat should use one reusable event mother engine before multiple mini-games.** Recommended first engine: `Task progress → Event currency → Milestone track → Collection/Decor reward → Optional Pass`, reskinned across multiple themes.
