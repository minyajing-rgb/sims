## What changed

1. **Priority Focus Product Cards are now 8/8.**
   - Existing deep cards: Gossip Harbor, Hay Day, Big Farm Homestead, Office Cat.
   - Added in v0.5: Township, Heartopia, The Cozy Florist, My Perfect Hotel.
   - Each card keeps Market/KPI, Art, Core, FTUE status, Progression/Meta, Economy, LiveOps, UA, Reviews/Versions, Trend/Cause, Production, Learn/Do-not-copy, Ops Cat Translation and gaps.

2. **FTUE is no longer uniformly TBD.**
   - Heartopia: PARTIAL public sequence evidence covering character creation → controls → starter home/furniture → first hobby → progression; external guide places Builder Tablet at roughly 10–15 minutes.
   - My Perfect Hotel: PARTIAL first-session sequence from first-party description + public beginner evidence: move/stand-to-act → service work → cash/tips → upgrades → staff automation.
   - The Cozy Florist walkthrough located but not yet timestamp-normalized.
   - Township timed new-user capture remains TBD.

3. **Township mature multi-engine LiveOps is now explicitly documented.**
   - Current US surfaces show Harvest Pass, Frozen Fortune, Guest from Space and Harvest Collection concurrently.
   - Current Regatta help: 12 tasks = 6 match-3 + 6 town tasks.
   - US App Store IAP surfaces include Tiny Cash, Piggy Bank, Golden Ticket and bundles/cash packs.
   - This is an orchestration benchmark, not a causal KPI claim.

4. **Heartopia is separated into architecture evidence vs KPI evidence.**
   - Hot Air Balloon and Friend Styling show social expression as live content.
   - MLP / build-challenge evidence is retained as LiveOps/brand architecture.
   - Download/revenue scope mismatch continues to block cross-scope RPD, LTV or IP-event ROI claims.

5. **The Cozy Florist August rebound remains UNEXPLAINED.**
   - High patch cadence is observable.
   - Clear `new content and events` notes become more explicit in September.
   - No paid/organic/source mix, ASO/store featuring, creative history or spend evidence exists, so v0.5 explicitly rejects an event-cause claim for August.

6. **My Perfect Hotel adds a new E1 Trend × Cause record.**
   - Repo Global collection data: June→July 2026 downloads +26.9%, IAP +63.6%.
   - US version history overlaps the rollout of Barbie Dream Hotel, including characters/skins, event pass and leaderboard.
   - Evidence level remains **E1**, not causal: market scope differs and source mix/spend/event participation are missing.

7. **Ops Cat Product Translation gains five explicit product rules.**
   - **Ownership anchor:** one persistent garden/home remains the identity; side games do not replace it.
   - **IP/theme wrapper:** reskin one reusable event area/pass without replacing the core.
   - **Social gating:** visits/styling/co-build come after ownership value exists.
   - **Ad guardrail:** forced-ad frequency and watch-to-progress pressure are soft-launch retention guardrails.
   - **Recovery P0:** paid events/rankings ship with progress persistence, purchase/reward reconciliation, compensation and recovery paths.
