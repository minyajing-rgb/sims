## 2. Township

### Identity / Market
- **OBSERVED · US:** 2.2M App Store ratings / 4.8 at capture; product explicitly combines city-building, farming, match-3, events, Regatta and collections.
- US App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/township/id638689075
- Current Playrix Regatta help: https://playrix.helpshift.com/hc/en/3-township/faq/16605-about-the-regatta-1765929812/

### Market / KPI
- **CALCULATED · Global collection scope:** visible 2021-09→2026-08 downloads ≈420.8M; IAP ≈$1.814B.
- **CALCULATED:** latest downloads ≈72.6% of visible 5Y peak while latest revenue is the visible 5Y peak.
- **INFERRED:** mature monetization resilience is much stronger than acquisition persistence.

### Art / Visual
- Isometric stylized 3D; highly modular town/farm/building/decor system.
- Strong mobile object readability and seasonal reskin capacity.
- Production class: **Very Heavy**, but with high engine/asset reuse.

### Core Gameplay
`Grow / Produce → Fulfill → Build / Expand Town → Match-3 for rewards → New Goals → Return`
- Core/Meta boundary is genuinely hybrid: town/farm ownership remains the persistent anchor while match-3 is deeply integrated.

### FTUE
- **TBD:** exact T+ timings require recorded US first-session capture.

### Progression / Meta / Side
- **OBSERVED current Regatta:** 12 tasks = 6 match-3 + 6 town tasks; team points drive race ranking.
- Collections, passes, expeditions/adventures, Regatta, new buildings and match-3 elements operate in parallel.

### Economy / Monetization
- **OBSERVED · US App Store IAP:** Tiny Cash Pack $1.99; Piggy Bank $2.99; Golden Ticket $4.99; cash packs and bundles up to $19.99 among visible products.
- **OBSERVED current Harvest Pass surface:** themed port/ship decor; Golden Ticket holders receive extra rewards and animal outfit benefits.
- Google Play marks purchases including random items.
- **TBD:** exact payer segmentation, pass cohort performance and source mix.

### LiveOps / Event Calendar
- **OBSERVED current US App Store concurrent surfaces:** Harvest Pass + Frozen Fortune + Guest from Space + Harvest Collection.
- This is four simultaneously visible event/promotion surfaces: pass/season, cooperative/partner progression, expedition, collection.
- **OBSERVED:** Frozen Fortune uses tasks → tokens → ship upgrades → rewards; Harvest Collection uses card-set completion → rewards/badge.

### Reviews / Versions / Quality
- App Store version notes bundle passes, adventures, card collection, Regatta seasons, new match-3 elements and buildings.
- **Review signal:** public US reviews include concern that match-3 difficulty/mandatory integration can overpower the original town/farm fantasy.
- Treat sentiment as a product-positioning warning, not causal proof.

### UA / Promotion
- Credible creative surfaces: town before/after, farming/production, match-3, expedition story, collections, seasonal pass, Regatta/social competition.
- Risk: presenting every mature system at once can dilute the first-session product promise.

### Production Burden
- Character: Heavy; Environment: Very Heavy; Event configuration: Very Heavy; Backend/social: Heavy; Reuse: High.
- Small-team fit is **Low-Medium** only as a system reference, not as a breadth target.

### Trend × Cause
- Existing Global lifecycle shows 2026 revenue strength while downloads move below peak.
- **E1 only for event/version overlap**; no US paid/organic, source mix or event participation, so current event stack cannot be called the cause of revenue strength.

### Learn
- Mature LiveOps can orchestrate multiple reusable engines around one persistent ownership anchor.
- Social competition can deliberately mix main-town and match-3 tasks.

### Do Not Copy
- Mature feature breadth in the MVP.
- Let side/minigame requirements eclipse the original ownership fantasy.
- Use calendar RPD as cohort LTV.

### Ops Cat Implication
- One persistent garden/home must remain the identity anchor even after adding minigames/events.
- If Ops Cat later adds team competition, split tasks between core ownership actions and optional side systems rather than making the side game mandatory.

### Gaps
US-only KPI/source mix; timed FTUE; exact pass segmentation; complete event-overlap calendar; historical review themes.

## 4. Heartopia

### Identity / Market
- **OBSERVED · US App Store:** multiplayer life sim centered on home building, six major hobbies, friends/co-building, 1000+ outfits and cats/dogs.
- US App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/heartopia/id6746151928
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.xd.xdtglobal.gp

- **CRITICAL:** downloads and revenue are not the same scope, so no cross-scope RPD / revenue persistence interpretation.

- Stylized free-camera 3D, fashion-forward avatar customization, large home/decor surface, pets, social scenes and hobby environments.

- Source: https://heartopia.gg/beginner-guide
- **TBD:** recorded US mobile timestamp sheet.

### Progression / Meta / Side
- Hobbies: fishing, cooking, gardening, birdwatching and others.
- Home/building, outfit collection, pet collection/behavior, friendship/social interactions, co-building, seasonal/event layers.
- **OBSERVED latest version:** Hot Air Balloon weekend event (Level 18+; up to six members) and Friend Styling (friend level 2+) turn sightseeing/customization into synchronous social activity.

### Economy / Monetization
- **OBSERVED · US App Store:** Heart Diamond ladder from $0.39 to $99.99; Junior/Formal Membership products also visible.
- Google Play labels in-game purchases including random items.
- Public review signals include gacha probability/duplicate/value complaints; treat as quality/value signals, not verified economy defects.

### LiveOps / Events
- **OBSERVED:** current US App Store season/event surfaces plus Hot Air Balloon and Friend Styling.
- **OBSERVED external official-partner evidence:** My Little Pony global collaboration ran in Feb–Mar 2026; collaboration used themed progression, collection/cosmetics and event rewards.
- Source: https://www.yodo1.com/press-releases/heartopia-welcomes-my-little-pony-in-a-global-re-launch-of-a-fan-favourite-collaboration

### Reviews / Versions / Quality
- Google Play current reviews include late-game repetition/content-depth concerns, gacha-value complaints and bug/maintenance complaints.
- Latest App Store version adds social-expression systems rather than a new isolated minigame.

### UA / Promotion
- Strong hooks: avatar/fashion, dream house, pets, hobby ASMR, friend co-building, social creativity, IP collaborations.

### Production Burden
- Character/fashion: Very Heavy; Environment: AAA-like; Animation/social: Very Heavy; monthly content: Very Heavy; backend/social: Heavy.
- Small-team fit: **Low** as a direct build target; high value as a 12–18 month ceiling/reference.

- **NOT CROSS-ATTRIBUTED:** current download and revenue series have scope mismatch.
- The MLP event is retained as a LiveOps/monetization architecture reference, not as a public-data causal KPI claim.

- Turn ownership/expression into the event engine itself: build, fashion, pet, social visit.
- Social features can be unlocked off relationship/progression gates rather than shown at D0.

### Do Not Copy
- Open-world/lifestyle breadth at MVP.
- Huge avatar/furniture catalog before a repeatable core is proven.
- Infer global event ROI from mismatched KPI scopes.

### Ops Cat Implication
- Use Heartopia as the long-term ceiling: after garden/home retention works, add co-visits, styling, pet and creator/build challenge layers.
- Do not attempt its world/content breadth in the first 6 months.

### Gaps
US-only KPI; same-scope revenue/downloads; timed mobile FTUE; event participation; gacha/offer screenshots; public team-size evidence.

## 8. My Perfect Hotel

### Identity / Market
- **OBSERVED · US App Store:** 702K ratings / 4.7 at current capture; fast-paced time-management/hotel empire positioning.
- US App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/my-perfect-hotel/id1635760774

### Market / KPI
- **CALCULATED · Global collection scope:** visible 2022-04→2026-08 downloads ≈338.9M; IAP ≈$28.8M.
- Latest downloads ≈26.7% of peak; latest IAP ≈38.8% of peak.
- June→July 2026: downloads +26.9%, IAP +63.6%; July→August: downloads +2.6%, IAP +4.4%.

### Art / Visual
- Functional stylized 3D; top-down/isometric service spaces; strong before/after capacity readability.
- Brand differentiation is weaker than flower/farm/lifestyle references, but D0 action clarity is excellent.

### Core Gameplay
`Move → Stand near task → Auto-complete service → Collect Cash/Tips → Upgrade Room/Facility/Staff → Expand Hotel → Return`
- Player starts extremely hands-on and gradually automates repetitive work through staff.

### FTUE
- **PARTIAL · official description:** starts as a bellhop cleaning rooms, greeting guests, collecting payments/tips and stocking bathrooms; upgrades rooms/facilities and hires staff as balance grows.
- **PARTIAL · external beginner guide:** control is move/stand-to-act and the first minutes focus on service → cash → first upgrades.
- **TBD:** recorded US mobile timestamps.

### Progression / Meta / Side
- Multiple hotels; rooms/facilities; staff; movement-speed upgrades; amenities; room designs/skins.
- 2026 versions add a major licensed IP event layer rather than replacing the core hotel loop.

### Economy / Monetization
- **OBSERVED · US App Store:** advertising is disclosed; visible IAP includes No Ads $9.99, gem packs, Daily Endless Offer, pet purchase and other packs.
- Public US reviews repeatedly flag forced-ad frequency and progression/grind pressure; treat as experience signals.

### LiveOps / Versions
- **OBSERVED:** `Barbie Dream Hotel` appears across June–September 2026 release notes.
- Event architecture: licensed IP hotel + character stories/skins + event pass + leaderboard character unlocks.
- 2025 history also shows seasonal adventure and leaderboard-point issue fixes, proving event/leaderboard is a reusable wrapper.

### Reviews / Quality
- Repeated US review themes: too many ads, lag/performance, high upgrade cost, progress-reset confusion in hotel upgrade flow.
- Developer explicitly responds that ads support free play; this confirms ads are intentional monetization, not only a complaint inference.

### UA / Promotion
- Credible hooks: one-finger service, empty→busy hotel, cash growth, staff automation, new hotel, licensed-IP takeover.
- Licensed IP gives a high-salience creative refresh without changing the underlying core.

### Production Burden
- Character: Low-Medium; Environment: Low-Medium modular; animation: Low; LiveOps/IP skinning: Medium; backend/social: Low-Medium.
- Small-team fit: **Very High** as a D0/core-production reference.

### Trend × Cause
- **E1:** June→July Global downloads +26.9% and IAP +63.6% overlaps the June launch and continued rollout of the Barbie Dream Hotel event/pass/leaderboard in US version history.
- **Counter evidence:** data is Global collection scope; no paid/organic, ad-revenue, source mix, IP marketing spend or event participation; version date is not proven activation date in every market.
- **Interpretation:** licensed IP + pass/leaderboard is a credible candidate driver / operating pattern, not a causal claim.

### Learn
- Extremely simple service action can support huge acquisition scale.
- Staff automation converts active D0 work into lighter management over time.
- Licensed IP can refresh creative and LiveOps without replacing the core.

### Do Not Copy
- High forced-ad pressure or grind that makes watching ads feel required to progress.
- Weak brand/ownership depth as the only long-term retention layer.
- Attribute July growth to Barbie without source-mix/event-participation evidence.

### Ops Cat Implication
- Borrow the D0 rule: movement/gesture should trigger immediate visible work and cash/resource feedback.
- After core validation, licensed/lifestyle themes can reskin a reusable event area/pass without rebuilding the game.
- Guardrail ad frequency and progression friction explicitly in soft launch.

### Gaps
US-only KPI/source mix; ad revenue/IAA split; exact event participation; timed FTUE; historical UA creatives/spend; IP campaign media evidence.
