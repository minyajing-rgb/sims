# Benchmark by Objective v1.9

Date: 2026-09-25  
Scope: US-first / Global-secondary

| Objective | Benchmark | Learn | Do not copy | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US short-window UA optimization | My Perfect Hotel | Use post-install purchase, ad-revenue and session events to optimize hybrid ROAS and scale | Relative lift is not absolute profitability | US Unity network case |
| US long-window UA optimization | Township | Use longer optimization windows to reach higher-value mature cohorts | D28-only bidding should not replace short-window guardrails | US Unity network case |
| Rewarded-engagement acquisition | Office Cat | Time/hidden quests can intentionally shape playtime and monetization | Do not use KR rewarded-retention figures as US organic targets | Korea Playio case |
| Localization-sensitive acquisition | Office Cat Japan | Channel/localization design can materially change CVR and retention shape | Do not merge KR and JP cohorts | Japan Playio case |
| Portfolio payer context | Heartopia / X.D. | Company-level MAU/payers provide commercial context when title data is missing | Do not assign portfolio payer ratio to Heartopia | X.D. 2026H1 portfolio evidence |
| KPI calibration discipline | US games aggregate | External benchmark, competitor case, prototype gate and scale gate must stay separate | Do not convert an external median into an owner target | External aggregate |
