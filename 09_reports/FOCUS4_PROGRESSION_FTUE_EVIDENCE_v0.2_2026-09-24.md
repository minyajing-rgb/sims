## External progression-gate evidence
A recent public strategy guide documents a repeated building progression pattern:

| Building / gate | External guide requirement | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| Building 1 → 2 | Player Lv 50 + 800,000 funds | OBSERVED-EXTERNAL-GUIDE |
| Building 2 → 3 | Lv 100 + 19,200,000 funds | OBSERVED-EXTERNAL-GUIDE |

Source: https://strat-vault.com/en/explanation-and-strategy-guide-for-each-building-in-the-area/

## Progression mechanism evidence
- **OBSERVED-EXTERNAL-GUIDE:** player level primarily rises through **company star upgrades** and **facility upgrades**.
- **OBSERVED-EXTERNAL-GUIDE:** office/tenant revenue is the economic anchor; high-rank companies require more EXP, and swapping companies can accelerate level gain.
- **REVIEW-SIGNAL:** players repeatedly describe electricity/power and gems as progression walls; one current review also reports tutorial interruption/skip friction.

Sources:
- https://strat-vault.com/en/basic-gameplay/
- https://strat-vault.com/en/priority-and-tricks-for-facility-level-up-the-truth-about-power-and-strikes/
- https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6471960010/office-cat-idle-tycoon-game/reviews

## What this closes
- Office Cat is no longer just a “lean modular 3D” reference; it now has an explicit **level + currency + company-star + facility-upgrade** progression model in the evidence stack.
- Exact D0 timing remains TBD.

## Product read
- **INFERRED:** Office Cat’s production efficiency comes partly from reusing the same room/company/upgrade grammar over many hard gates.
- **Do not copy:** large currency cliffs (e.g. multi-billion building gates), power walls, and premium-dependent morale/decor pressure without cohort validation.
- **Ops Cat implication:** use modular room/area grammar, but keep each new area tied to a visible new ownership fantasy—not only a larger number requirement.

# 5. Cross-product progression pattern

## Pattern A — D0 clarity and long meta should be separate layers
- My Perfect Hotel = strongest D0 action clarity.
- Office Cat = repeated hard progression / modular building grammar.
- Big Farm Homestead = first-party staged region/social unlocks.
- Cozy Florist = later cosmetic/collection gate on top of garden/shop core.

## Pattern B — Social is rarely the first reason to stay
Evidence now supports a staged approach rather than front-loading community systems:
- Big Farm Homestead explicitly gates Community Island/guild at Level 12.
- Cozy Florist’s outfit/cosmetic layer is reported at Level 10.
- Office Cat’s early/mid progression is dominated by tenant/company/facility economics, not social breadth.

## Pattern C — content burden should scale after ownership value
- **INFERRED:** Ops Cat should first prove one persistent garden/home + satisfying action before adding guild, visits, outfit collections, or heavy live event content.
