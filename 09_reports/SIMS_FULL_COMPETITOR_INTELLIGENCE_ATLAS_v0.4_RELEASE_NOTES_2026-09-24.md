Each card explicitly covers Identity/Market, Business Trend, Art/Visual, Core, FTUE status, Progression/Meta/Side, Economy/Monetization, LiveOps, Versions/Reviews/Quality, UA/Creative, Production Burden, Trend×Cause, Learn, Do-not-copy, Ops Cat implication and evidence gaps.
- Added `BENCHMARK_BY_OBJECTIVE_v0.2`: durable revenue, new cozy/farming monetization, narrative LiveOps, lean production, D0 readability, lifestyle ownership ceiling, art/brand, LiveOps architecture, D2C/loyalty and creative-truthfulness risk now use different benchmark sets instead of one generic ranking.
- Added `PRODUCTION_BURDEN_MATRIX_v0.2`: comparative character/environment/animation/VFX/narrative/LiveOps/backend burden, reuse ratio and small-team fit for eight focus titles.
- Added **Hay Day D2C / recurring pass architecture** from official Supercell Store/support evidence: Farm Pass, store rewards, store-exclusive offers and selected-region Farm Pass Bundle Subscription with seasonal renewal and capacity/choice-crate perks.
- Added **Big Farm Homestead operations-quality evidence** from its official blog: a Festival Pass issue was publicly acknowledged and investigated; community/Discord voucher milestones are also observable. This upgrades monetized-event recovery/support from a generic recommendation to an evidence-backed operating requirement.
- Added **Gossip Harbor creative-truthfulness / economy-pressure risk** from App Store review signals: energy scarcity, event/card completion pressure and misleading-ad expectation gaps. These remain review signals, not confirmed causal or algorithmic claims.
- Added Ops Cat P0 LiveOps requirement: event progress persistence, purchase/reward reconciliation, ranking-reset safety, compensation and recovery rules before scaling monetized events.

## Current strongest reads

1. **Hay Day is now the strongest durable-value / D2C reference, not only a farming-core reference.** Its current public architecture shows recurring pass + D2C + loyalty/capacity perks layered on an intact evergreen production/trade loop.
2. **Big Farm Homestead is the strongest newer farming LiveOps/monetization teardown candidate.** Calendar revenue holds much better than downloads after the visible peak, while Festival/Pass/event/collection systems are highly visible. This is still not cohort retention proof.
3. **Gossip Harbor is the strongest narrative operating-system reference but also the clearest creative/economy pressure caution.** Weekly story and reusable event wrappers coexist with repeated energy/event-completion and ad-expectation review signals.
4. **Office Cat remains the best lean modular-3D production benchmark, not a long-term durability benchmark.** Content/events can support the remaining base without reversing acquisition decay.
5. **Ops Cat should design event recovery as part of the product system.** A monetized event is not complete without persistence/recovery/compensation/support rules.
