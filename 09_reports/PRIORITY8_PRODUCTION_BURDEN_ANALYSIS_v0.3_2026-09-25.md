## Evidence rule

- Exact game-team size is recorded only when public.
- Company headcount is labeled company-level and never converted into title-team size.
- Historical team size stays HISTORICAL.
- Production burden is not inferred from visual richness alone; use code/system reuse, time-to-content, live-ops scope, localization/IP approvals, QA and creative throughput.

## New evidence

### My Perfect Hotel
- **SOURCE / HISTORICAL:** a 2023 SayGames interview described the developer team as roughly half a dozen developers during the early period.
- **SOURCE / CURRENT LIVEOPS:** the title now operates third-party IP integration plus seasonal LiveOps and additional hotel content.
- **Decision:** lean core and heavier publisher/live-service tooling can coexist. Do not assume the historical team size is current.

### Hay Day
- **SOURCE / HISTORICAL:** public retrospective reporting places the live team around 13–15 people in 2013–14, briefly 17 around Android launch.
- **PROCESS READ:** the team later narrowed update quantity for better balance and quality.
- **Decision:** a small expert team can sustain a hit, but long-lived LiveOps accumulates systems and QA.

### Township
- **SOURCE / HISTORICAL:** public anniversary reporting places the team around 20 at launch and around 130 by 2023, with 50+ events over the first decade.
- **Decision:** Township is a mature-operations benchmark, not an MVP staffing template.

### Big Farm Homestead
- **SOURCE / CURRENT 2026:** Unity says the title was delivered in under a year by building on sister-game learnings.
- **SOURCE / CURRENT 2026:** New Moon Production hiring material says Big Farm Homestead shares codebase and many systems with Sunshine Island, and sits inside a 100+ person BIG! franchise organization.
- **Decision:** shared code, UI, feature and event systems are concrete production-speed levers; exact BFH title-team size remains TBD.

### The Cozy Florist
- **SOURCE / CURRENT:** Japan launch material documents professional localization, local flower content and device tuning.
- **SOURCE / EXTERNAL CURRENT 30D:** an external creative tracker reports 2,291 tracked creatives and 6,939 ad plans during 2026-08-17→09-16, with a vendor-defined 48.7% reuse flag.
- **Decision:** acquisition creative throughput is a separate operating system from product content. Do not copy volume before PMF.

### Heartopia
- **SOURCE / COMPANY-LEVEL:** XD reported expanding Heartopia's development team; company game-development staff were 724 at end-2024. Exact Heartopia team size is unavailable.
- **SOURCE / GLOBAL OPS:** the 2026 global IP relaunch reused China event assets but added localization approvals, brand consistency and music licensing.
- **Decision:** asset reuse can reduce creative risk while IP/global approvals add project-management burden.

### Office Cat
- Current exact title-team size remains **TBD**.
- Modular rooms, furniture and character production remain the strongest evidence for lean 3D throughput.
- Corporate merger information is context only; it is not a team-size proxy.

### Gossip Harbor
- Current exact title-team size remains **TBD**.
- The publisher operates multiple live merge titles, supporting portfolio/system reuse.
- Some additional-store distribution is handled by an external distribution partner.
- Narrative/event content burden remains high.

## Ops Cat translation

1. Prototype with one readable action + one owned space.
2. Build modular environment / UI / event kits before bespoke breadth.
3. Build one reusable event mother-engine before frequent client releases.
4. Track **asset-hours per shipped feature/event** and **winner-rate per creative asset-hour**.
5. Delay IP/global physical-reward operations until D7 retention and monetization proof.
6. Do not benchmark our team against mature Township/Heartopia organization scale.
