## Strongest new reads

- **Creative volume is an operating signal, not a business-quality score.** Hay Day has a much smaller creative-volume proxy than Township while remaining near its visible five-year revenue peak.
- **Big Farm Homestead + My Perfect Hotel form the strongest combined acquisition template for Ops Cat:** persistent `home` ownership promise + immediate readable action.
- **Heartopia proves social-expression reach, not durable retention.** Social identity remains a later ceiling after owned-space attachment.
- **The Cozy Florist supports a lifestyle/female-first creative hypothesis, not a demographic targeting rule.** Card-level audience data cannot be generalized to the whole product.
- **ADX closes the UA/Creative evidence gap, not the attribution gap.** Spend, CTR/CVR, paid/organic and matched creative time series remain TBD.
