Executive

Current US: 4.6 / 205K ratings / #15 Casual / 318.3MB; current visible iOS 3.99.0. Gossip Harbor is the highest-commercial-scale title in the 19-game table. Its cheap Merge-2 core is wrapped by weekly story, restaurant renovation, long card/pass spines, dense short-event rails and a massive creative operation.

Market/KPI

Same-scope Global Diandian: Aug-2026 = 9.53M downloads / $87.03M IAP / calendar pseudo-ARPI 9.13. Dec→Jan acquisition more than doubled while value diluted; Feb→Mar IAP jumped 28.1%; Jun→Jul downloads +61.3%; Jul→Aug downloads -16.1% while IAP +9.7%.

Art

Portrait merge board + polished illustrated story/renovation scenes. Character emotion and visible transformation are marketing assets. Store screenshot sequence: Help Them → Story → New Life → Renovate → Design → Merge. Rendering burden is lower than 3D Sims, but narrative/event illustration throughput is high.

Core

Energy → generator → merge → order → Coins/XP → Chore Book story/renovation → new orders/generators → return. The board remains the resource-production core; story/renovation is the motivation/meta layer.

FTUE

HIGH-PARTIAL. Current guides confirm visible-order-first play, 6–8 empty tiles before generator taps, 1 Energy / 2 min and meaningful board pressure around Level 12. Exact current T+0→30m, first event/card/pass/offer/ad timing remains TBD.

Progression/Meta/Side

Story/renovation, generator upgrades, inventory, persistent Special Requests, 39-day Card Albums + Star Store duplicate sink + Prestige, month-long Seasons, Restaurant Goals, 24h competitions, coin/energy/point/decoration events and multiple alternate merge/exploration event families.

Economy/Monetization

Energy = throughput; Coins = story/renovation; Gems = premium utility; Board Space = frictional capacity; Cards/Duplicates = collection value. Current US visible IAP: Gems $1.99/$4.99, Premium Energy $3.99, Event Packs $2.99–$3.99, Coffee $2.99, Seafood $3.99, Super Pass IV $7.99.

LiveOps

Current Nova Morel Voyage + Turning Leaves context. Recent cycles: Mid-Autumn mystery, Raccoon Beach, dragon rescue, Dollhouse, Ancient Egypt/Coconut, Global Goblet. App Store explicitly promises NEW EPISODE EVERY FRIDAY. Long album/season rails overlap short event engines.

UA/Creative

SocialPeta observed 14.7K creatives / 30d, 17 channels, 253 countries/regions; AppLovin 55%, AdMob 18%, Vungle 8% of observed activity. This is not spend share. Emotional crisis/story/transformation creative is a major hook family; current US reviews still flag ad-promise mismatch.

E0/E1 only. March monetization jump and July acquisition jump remain driver-unresolved. August is an installed-base monetization candidate because downloads normalize while IAP reaches a new repository-series high. No causal event attribution.

Reviews/Versions

Current review themes: pop-up clutter, album duplicate/completion frustration, long Energy waits and misleading acquisition ads; positive story/decor and failed-ad stamina compensation. Visible Jun24→Sep22 cadence: 10 versions; median 9d, mean 10d.

Focus Game

Connected LiveOps is the core benchmark. The same Energy/order action can advance story, Season, Card Album, 24h goals and event progress. This increases value density without inventing a new core mechanic for each event, at the cost of high operational complexity and resource-pressure risk.

Production Burden

Heavy overall. Weekly narrative + story illustration + localization + many event engines + 1–2 week client cadence + 14.7K current observed creatives. 2D lowers rendering burden but does not make the operation lean. Title FTE and asset/config/QA/localization hours remain TBD.
