### Offerwall cohort contamination
The observed cohort is NOT an organic cohort.

The guide says recent comparison players were completing Freecash offers. A special external reward of about **$54.2** was available for reaching Spring Scent within 7 days, which produced unusually aggressive early play/spending. About half of the sample reportedly gave up that 7-day bonus and switched to the normal 50-day goal.

Therefore:
- days-to-building ≠ organic progression median
- spend→days sample ≠ payer elasticity
- screen time ≠ normal session behavior
- offerwall must be tagged as its own acquisition/source cohort
