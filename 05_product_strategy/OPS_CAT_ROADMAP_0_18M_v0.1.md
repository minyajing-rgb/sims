# Ops Cat 0–18 Month Product Roadmap v0.1

## Phase 0 — 0 to 6 weeks
**Goal:** prove one satisfying action and one ownership transformation.

Build:
- 1 yard,
- mowing,
- cleaning,
- resource pickup,
- 3 visual yard states,
- 15–30 decoration objects,
- one short personalized story chain.

No:
- open world,
- complex social,
- full 3D city,
- large NPC cast.

## Phase 1 — 6 weeks to 3 months
**Goal:** playable web/H5 test.

Add:
- preference onboarding,
- personalized yard seed,
- 3 NPC archetypes,
- 20–30 story modules,
- tool upgrades,
- collection page,
- event hooks,
- analytics.

## Phase 2 — 3 to 6 months
**Goal:** retention + creative validation.

Run:
- art-style A/B/C,
- video creative test,
- paid traffic,
- cohort analysis.

Gate:
- tutorial completion,
- D1,
- D3,
- D7,
- repeat decoration,
- story-choice interaction.

## Phase 3 — 6 to 12 months
If the loop works, add:
- second environment,
- home / room,
- pets,
- stronger NPC relationships,
- season / album,
- light social,
- richer AI memory.

## Phase 4 — 12 to 18 months
Only if retention and LTV justify:
- travel world,
- water-sports event line,
- UGC / visits,
- community spaces,
- optional light roguelite / skill mini-game layer,
- native store packaging and broader platform release.
