## Updated art direction
The clean, bright, toy-like 3D look is readable and market-friendly but risks becoming generic.

New target:
> **Bright cozy base + 10–30% handcrafted authorial layer**

Keep:
- high readability
- warmth
- optimistic lighting
- clear interactable objects
- friendly saturated palette

Add:
- hand-painted edge treatment
- gouache / watercolor-like surface variation
- imperfect brush texture
- illustrated signs, notes and postcards
- more narrative props
- more personal environmental details
- stronger sense that someone lives here

Avoid:
- plastic toy rendering everywhere
- generic AI-cute sameness
- decorative beauty with too little gameplay
- overly dark realism

## Current product thesis
Not Pure Casual versus Full Simulation.

Use:
> **Casual acquisition hook + satisfying core action + light simulation ownership + AI-personalized narrative**

## AI-native direction
AI should change the player world, not only act as a chatbot:
- preference onboarding changes the starting yard / home
- player goals influence task selection
- NPC requests and small story arcs vary
- generated text and selected/generated images support personalized scenes
- memory tracks preferences, favorite spaces, characters, collections and prior choices

Deterministic game systems still control:
- economy
- progression
- unlock conditions
- rewards
- safety limits
- asset budgets

AI controls:
- narrative variation
- scene flavor
- NPC dialogue
- personalized task framing
- next-story selection
- cosmetic combinations within approved asset sets
