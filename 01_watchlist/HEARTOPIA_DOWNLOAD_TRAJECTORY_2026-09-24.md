# Heartopia Global download trajectory

Source: Diandian monthly export, pulled 2026-09-24. Global scope equals App Store ID `6746151928` plus Google Play package `com.xd.xdtglobal.gp`; China App Store ID `1561903786` is excluded.

## Verified result

- Store listing month: 2025-12. Diandian reports zero global downloads in that month.
- Formal launch and download peak: 2026-01, with 10,886,824 downloads.
- 2026-08 downloads: 385,481, or 3.54% of the January peak.
- From January to August, monthly downloads fell 96.46%.
- iOS share rose from 49.32% in January to 59.08% in August as Google Play declined faster.

This confirms a sharp launch spike followed by sustained download normalization. It does not establish what happened to organic volume or revenue: the paid or organic export was not collected, and Diandian's monthly income page failed to finish loading during this pass. Those conclusions remain open.

The normalized rows are in `DIANDIAN_MONTHLY_HEARTOPIA_GLOBAL_2025-12_2026-08.csv`. The licensed raw export is retained locally and is not committed to the public repository.
