# Diandian Priority Watchlist v0.2 — Cozy / Casual-to-Long-Meta

**Date:** 2026-09-24

## What this list is for
This is the prioritized title list for Codex to pull from DianDian / AppMagic-style sources.

Focus:
- cozy sims,
- casual nurturing / management,
- mini-games,
- one-touch or low-friction entry,
- long progression / meta,
- recent black horses,
- big-publisher new bets,
- structural baselines.

## Pull order
1. **P0**: download first.
2. **P1**: second batch / baseline.
3. **P2**: trend radar or non-mobile references.

## Required monthly fields
For every P0/P1 title:
- month
- total downloads
- iOS downloads
- Google Play downloads
- revenue
- paid downloads / paid traffic estimate
- organic downloads / organic share
- top 5 countries
- ad creative count / spend proxy if available
- rating / review count
- major version
- liveops event / collab
- launch-month index

Derived:
- Organic/Paid ratio
- Revenue per Download
- Revenue persistence vs download decline
- post-UA active/revenue persistence where available
